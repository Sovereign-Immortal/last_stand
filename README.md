# ⚰️ LAST STAND

> **"You were buried alive. The world you wake to is already dead — and it wants you to join it."**

---

## 🩸 What Is Last Stand?

**Last Stand** is a top-down 2D survival shooter built in Godot 4, where every wave of enemies brings you closer to a truth you were never meant to remember.

You play as **Kaelan** — a young man who wakes from a stone sarcophagus with no memory, no name, and no idea why the dead are crawling toward him. As you fight through ruin, graveyard, and underground corridor, fragments of a buried history surface: suppressed memories, military logs, ancient inscriptions, and whispers from a civilization of giants that predates humanity itself.

You are not just a survivor. You are the last prince of a forgotten bloodline — and something deep underground is waiting for you to come home.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔫 Deep Combat System
- **3 weapons** — Pistol (infinite ammo), Machine Gun (heavy but devastating), Silencer (high damage, precision)
- **5 bullet types** — Standard · Quick (1.6× speed) · Paralysis (stun) · Knockback · Slow Down — each with limited ammo and a distinct tactical role
- **5 throwables** — Grenade · Landmine · Ice Bomb · Skill Point Orb · **Giantification** (temporarily transform into a colossal shockwave engine)
- Kill combo multiplier (up to 5×) for score and EXP

### 🗺️ 4 Unique Maps
| Zone | Environment | Unlock Condition |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Sovereign Ruins** | Open graveyard around the Giant King's sarcophagus | Always available |
| **Cemetery Hills** | Tombstone maze with crypts and cover | Defeat 2 bosses in Zone 1 |
| **Subway Tunnels** | Tight underground corridors, heavy echo filter | Defeat 2 bosses in both Zone 1 & 2 |
| **Heart Cavern** | Pulsing crimson core of the world | Story progression |

### 👾 Enemy Roster
- **Standard Zombie** — shambling horde filler
- **Cyborg Zombie** — ex-soldier trapped in broken defense protocols
- **Runner** — chemical-engine speed freak
- **Bomber** — volatile, suicidal, and relentless
- **Zombified Giant** — a titan from a dead era, too stubborn to stop walking
- **True Boss (Subject 0)** — the Spine, Skull, and Heart of a giant rebuilt into a killing machine

### 🧠 RPG Progression
- Earn EXP from kills → level up → allocate skill points
- Three stats to upgrade: **HP · Speed · Damage**
- Hire **mercenaries** (Hunter or Pacifist) from the in-game merchant
- Persistent cross-map progress — your weapons and ammo carry between zones

### 📖 Lore System — The Archive of Truth
31 discoverable lore fragments, sorted across three pillars:
- **Implanted** — the fake memories they built inside your skull
- **Giant Heritage** — who you actually are, and what your bloodline did
- **World** — how humanity cracked open the old world and let the dead walk out

Reading conflicting lore fragments triggers real gameplay consequences — including a tinnitus flashback and Kaelan breaking down mid-run.

### 🏁 5 Endings
The final choice at the Heart Cavern is yours:
| Ending | Nature |
|--------|--------|
| **Destroy** | Obliterate the Heart. No more giants. No more infection. No more Kaelan. |
| **Absorb** | Consume it. Become something that is no longer human. |
| **Seal** | Lock it away again. Buy the world more time. |
| **Veil's End** | Dissolve the barrier entirely. Let both worlds bleed into each other. |
| **Truest True Ending** | Only unlocked if you've seen everything. The throne was never the Giant King's to begin with. |

## 🎮 Controls

### Desktop (Keyboard + Mouse)
| Action | Key |
|--------|-----|
| Move | WASD |
| Aim & Shoot | Mouse — Left Click |
| Reload | R |
| Interact | E |
| Cycle Bullet Type | Q (forward) / R (backward) |
| Cycle Explosive | F |
| Throw Explosive | X |
| Switch Weapon | 1 / 2 or Scroll Wheel |
| Weapon Inventory | G |
| Character Menu | C |
| Mercenary Menu | V |
| Pause | Escape |

### Android / Touch
| Action | Control |
|--------|---------|
| Move | Left virtual joystick (drag anywhere left 40% of screen) |
| Aim & Shoot | Touch and drag on right side of screen |
| All actions | On-screen button grid (bottom right) |

## 🏗️ Built With

- **Engine:** [Godot 4.6](https://godotengine.org/) — GL Compatibility renderer
- **Language:** GDScript
- **Architecture:** Autoloaded globals, signal-driven HUD, wave manager, programmatic UI
- **Audio:** Adaptive audio system with tinnitus, headache echo filter, and positional volume
- **Shaders:** Custom vignette, muzzle flash, and circle UI shader — all written inline
